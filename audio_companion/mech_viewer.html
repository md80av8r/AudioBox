Hover a part
Teal = daughterboard parts (heights from their 3D models). Blue = CrowPanel parts; dark = measured, pale = estimated. Red = collision. Grey = case (walls, rear cover, top plate with display window, corner screw bosses outside the board outline). Round markers on the top plate show where each control sits: red = behind the display, amber = behind the CrowPanel board, green = outside it (reachable through the bezel). Display size and position, display thickness and CrowPanel position (about 2 mm) are estimates. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
